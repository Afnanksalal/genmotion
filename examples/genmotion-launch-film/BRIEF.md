# Genmotion launch film

## Job

Convince motion designers, creative developers, and product teams that Genmotion is a serious native motion system an agent can direct, inspect, revise, and render.

## One-line promise

Direct the scene. Keep every decision.

## Proof

- The real Genmotion Studio edits the same Creative IR consumed by the renderer.
- Workflow, editor, timeline, validation, and export are shown with captured product pixels.
- Typography, paths, perspective planes, blur, compositing, and sound remain authored and deterministic.
- The final film itself is rendered by Genmotion.

## Desired action

Open the repository and create a first project.

## Creative rule

Treat the product as a spatial instrument. The camera travels through one connected scene system; it does not page through slides or a grid of feature cards.

## Delivery

- 1920 × 1080, 30 fps
- 45.453 seconds for the current authored cut
- Music and sound design, no voiceover
- Dark graphite field, bone-white type, Genmotion blue focus (`#60a5fa`), and deep brand-blue light (`#2563eb`)
