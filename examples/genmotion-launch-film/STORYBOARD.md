# Genmotion launch film storyboard

## Story

An agent begins with intent. Genmotion turns that intent into an editable scene graph, exposes the same structure in Studio, directs its layers over time, and resolves them through the native renderer into a verified master.

The film follows one object: the four-part Genmotion mark. Its pieces become nodes, the graph becomes the Studio workflow, the workflow becomes the editor timeline, and the directed layers return to the mark in the exported frame.

No decorative rail, slash, card, particle, or synthetic UI annotation appears over the product captures.

## Transition logic

1. The mark and product premise motivate the opening handoff.
2. Spatial continuity connects the abstract system to the real Workflow and Editor captures.
3. Timeline position motivates the layer-premise cut.
4. The premise occupies its own readable scene before the diagonal capability flight.
5. The capability planes resolve into the native payoff without exposing incompatible scene layouts.
6. The payoff settles before the call to action crossfades in.

## Hard exclusions

- No ambient diagonal rails or unrelated slash lines.
- No decorative particles or arbitrary perspective cards.
- No transparent Studio capture or uncovered frame corner.
- No synthetic connector or marker over captured product controls.
- No caption plate or caption overlap on essential interface content.
- No camera move before the previous move settles.
- No modification of the approved line `EVERY LAYER MOVES THE STORY`.
- No flattening of the intentional diagonal capability arrangement.
