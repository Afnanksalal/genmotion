# Reference direction

## Observed qualities

The supplied launch-film reference uses a dark spatial stage, very large restrained type, small technical metadata, connected object fields, and camera-led reveals. Its shots feel related because graphic lines and object trajectories continue across edits.

## Adaptation

Genmotion uses those broad motion-design qualities to explain its own product truth:

- Floating abstract tiles become authored Creative IR planes and real Studio captures.
- Network lines become the route from brief to scene graph to verified master.
- Macro technical typography names real Genmotion capabilities.
- The palette, brand mark, layouts, copy, captured interface, timing, and scene construction are original to Genmotion.

## Rejected patterns

- Equal feature-card grids
- Fake dashboards or generated UI
- Constant 3D rotation with no narrative target
- Generic gradient blobs and decorative particles
- Subtitle-style captions centered at the bottom
- Music selected after picture lock
