# Taste and reference use

Genmotion references are abstract decision records, not footage or templates.

Use at least two references from distinct families. For each direction, write:

- `borrow`: the structural or rhythmic principle worth carrying forward;
- `avoid`: recognizable surface features, common defaults, or irrelevant behavior that must not appear;
- `transform`: how the principle changes for this product, audience, and proof.

Reject a direction when its identity depends on one reference, its surface can be described only with a fashionable brand name, or a different product could replace the current product without changing the scene.

Generate several cheap directions before committing. Compare them pairwise for hierarchy, clarity, originality, brand fit, rhythm, and feasibility. Do not trust a single self-assigned aesthetic score.

Use `.genmotion/concepts.json` as an auditable creative decision record. Preserve the selected concept ID in project metadata.
